## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a high-velocity, precision-engineered telemetry aesthetic designed for demanding technical platforms, high-performance computing, and elite conditioning dashboards. It merges deep obsidian dark minimalism with electric amber-gold illumination, creating a tactical atmosphere that feels focused, powerful, and deliberate.

The visual style combines crisp tonal dark surfaces with selective golden luminescence and translucent cybernetic layers. It rejects superfluous ornamentation in favor of purposeful high-contrast utility: pitch-black canvases frame structural slate modules, while luminous warm accents guide focus, denote status transitions, and elevate critical system telemetry.

## Colors

The color system anchors in an obsidian void, replacing cool blues with an electric golden yellow spectrum that communicates velocity, focus, and energy without inducing fatigue.

- **Obsidian Foundation:** The canvas rests on `#0A0E16` (deepest base) transitioning to `#181C24` for standard card enclosures and `#262A33` for elevated or floating modules.
- **Electric Golden Highlights:**
  - **Primary (`#F59E0B`):** Warm amber gold used for core interactive elements, key telemetry callouts, and primary focal targets.
  - **Secondary (`#FACC15`):** High-voltage electric yellow designated for active status indicators, micro-highlights, and high-energy alert points.
  - **Tertiary (`#D97706`):** Deep burnished amber providing depth to gradients, hover states, and ambient glow envelopes.
- **Typography & Structure:** Crisp readability is maintained through high-contrast off-whites (`#DFE2EE`) for primary data and muted cool grays (`#94A3B8`) for secondary labels and units.
- **Radiance & Borders:** Boundaries are defined through sub-perceptual hairline strokes (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) and targeted amber luminescence (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.3)`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the bold, technical geometry of `Space Grotesk` with the balanced, utilitarian legibility of `Inter`.

- **Headlines & Display:** `Space Grotesk` provides a deliberate mechanical aesthetic. Display and section titles leverage tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and heavy weights to convey engineering rigor and forward motion.
- **Prose & Extended Reading:** `Inter` handles all body paragraphs, narrative text, and form inputs. Its neutral proportions ensure optimal legibility across varying viewports and dense dashboard layouts.
- **Labels, Telemetry & Metadata:** Set in `Space Grotesk` uppercase with expanded letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.12em`). This creates crisp, instrument-grade labels suitable for telemetry metrics, status tags, and table headers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a fluid 12-column grid system built on an 8-point harmonic rhythm. 

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop displays distribute content across 12 fluid columns with `1.5rem` gutters. Tablet devices step down to an 8-column or 6-column layout, and mobile interfaces collapse to a unified 4-column flow with `1rem` gutters.
- **Canvas Margins:** Generous external margins (`3.5rem` desktop, shrinking to `1.25rem` on mobile) isolate content areas from viewport bounds, reinforcing the impression of elevated consoles floating over deep space.
- **Internal Padding & Gaps:** Structural modules employ `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) for interior boundaries, ensuring dense telemetry readouts do not bleed into edge borders. Micro-spacing (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) separates inline labels from their corresponding values.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects heavy, opaque drop shadows in favor of tonal surface stepping, translucent glassmorphism, and radiant golden backlights.

- **Tonal Stepping:** The canvas sits at `#0A0E16`. Primary content modules step up to `#181C24`, while elevated panels, dropdowns, and overlays sit at `#262A33`.
- **Low-Contrast Ghost Outlines:** Cards and modules feature a delicate 1px perimeter border using `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. This separates deep slate containers without hard visual interruption.
- **Amber Glow Envelopes:** High-priority elements project an electric warm radiance:
  - Primary button hover: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px -2px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.4)`
  - Active telemetry focus: `box-shadow: 0 0 16px 0 rgba(250, 204, 21, 0.25)`
  - System warning or critical emphasis: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.35)`
- **Glassmorphic Paneling:** Sticky navbars and HUD elements employ backdrop blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) over translucent slate (`rgba(24, 28, 36, 0.75)`), allowing underlying movement and data streams to filter through subtly.

## Shapes

The design system implements a disciplined soft geometry (`roundedness: 1`). Corner radii are held tight to reinforce an architectural, high-precision aesthetic.

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px):** Applied to form inputs, list items, small cards, tooltips, and checkboxes.
- **Large Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to primary structural cards, dashboards, and modal viewports.
- **Extra-Large Radius (0.75rem / 12px):** Reserved for floating dialogs and heavy overlay panels.
- **Functional Pills (9999px):** Status badges, chips, and primary call-to-action buttons selectively leverage fully rounded pill geometry, creating immediate distinction between informational enclosures and interactive triggers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** High-impact button with a solid `#F59E0B` fill, text set in `#0A0E16` `Space Grotesk` (semi-bold/bold), and subtle corner rounding (`0.25rem` or pill-shaped for CTAs). Hover states introduce an electric yellow aura (`0 0 18px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary (Glass):** Translucent container (`rgba(38, 42, 51, 0.6)`) with a 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`). Hovering triggers an amber border highlight (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.5)`) and shifts typography toward `#FACC15`.
- **Ghost / Minimal:** Transparent background with `#DFE2EE` label; active state glows with a faint amber underline.

### Cards & Telemetry Containers
- **Structure:** Surface rendered in `#181C24` backed by a 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`). Outer corners set to `0.5rem`.
- **Header:** Features a `label-md` uppercase category in `#94A3B8` paired with a numeric readout or title in `Space Grotesk`.
- **Accent Lines:** Optional gradient accent line (`1px` top border transitioning from `#F59E0B` to transparent) to highlight high-velocity cards.

### Input Fields
- **Base State:** Matte obsidian fill (`#0A0E16`) with a 1px outline in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Typography set in `Inter` 14px (`#DFE2EE`).
- **Focus State:** Outline transitions to `#F59E0B` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 10px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`). Placeholder text rests in `#64748B`.

### Chips & Status Badges
- Compact pill-shaped containers (`rounded-full`) with a low-opacity amber background (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.1)`).
- Typography set in `label-sm` uppercase `#FACC15`. Features an optional 6px circular indicator dot pulsing with an ambient gold glow.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Squared (`0.25rem` radius) or circular controls framed with a 1px stroke in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.
- Selected state fills with solid `#F59E0B` containing a dark `#0A0E16` vector checkmark, accompanied by a micro-radiance halo.

### Lists & Data Rows
- Alternating subtle row divisions (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`) with interactive hover states shifting row background to `#262A33`.
- Left-edge indicator shifts to an electric amber vertical pip (`2px` width) upon selection or focus.
